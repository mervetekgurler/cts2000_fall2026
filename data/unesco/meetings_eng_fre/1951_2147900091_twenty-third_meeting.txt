[1] Mr. THOMPSON (United Kingdom) explained that his delegation’s resolution was largely concerned with the staffing of the unit. The programme of the Rehabilitation Service had received general approval and his delegation’s revision of the work plan was merely a question of drafting. The real problem was to determine’ the number of staff required to carry out such a programme. Mr. Thompson proposed to defer consideration of the Office of the Head and to begin with the actual operational Divisions, as indicated on page 2 of document 6C/5(II) Corr. IV. He understood from the Head of the Service that the functions of the three Divisions shown were the following. Coupon Programme: The purely administrative control of the issue of coupons and the finances of the Gift Coupon Scheme. Direct Operations: Three sub-divisions existed, of which the first, “Special Projects and Information on Needs”, collected detailed information from the programme Departments and outside bodies, which it prepared in the form of Gift Coupon projects. The other two sub-divisions were concerned with special projects affecting the Middle East and Korea respectively. Promotion of Voluntary Assistance: Upon receipt of the information prepared by the Direct Operations unit, the Division worked it into publicity material with the best psychological appeal for distribution to Member States and donor groups. [2] It was that Division which the United Kingdom delegation considered over-staffed. It maintained a New York office of five persons, whilst a further staff of seven existed in Paris to supply the neces- sary publicity materials. Mr. Thompson understood that two headquarters staff members were employed on the preparation of material for the New York office, while a further two performed the same service for European countries. His delegation felt that, in view of the existing New York office, and the fact that European agencies were well-equipped to handle such work, the staff of the Division could be reduced by two assistants at Grade 10 and one secretary at Grade 4.
