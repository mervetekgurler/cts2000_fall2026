(1.1) The President (translation from the Arabic):   Ladies and gentlemen, I declare open the fourteenth plenary meeting of the General Conference. Before I announce  the results of the elections to the Executive Board, I should like to inform you that the Bureau of the General Conference held  its third meeting on Tuesday morning yesterday. The Bureau examined the initial reports of the work of the commission and  committees. I am pleased to tell you that their work has been completed within the deadlines. As you know, during the meeting  of the Education (ED) Commission on Friday 3 November the concerned parties reached an agreement on agenda item 4.3  “Implementation of 38 C/Resolution 72 concerning educational and cultural institutions in the occupied Arab territories”.  According to the terms of the agreement, the draft resolution was approved unanimously by the ED Commission. The  concerned parties also agreed to withdraw the agenda item from the agenda of the Culture (CLT) Commission at the present  session of the General Conference. The Bureau of the General Conference was also consulted about the matter at its meeting  of 6 November. The Bureau of the General Conference recommended the withdrawal of agenda item 4.3 from the agenda of  the CLT Commission. May I take it that you agree with this recommendation? If there is no objection, it is therefore decided to  withdraw item 4.3 “Implementation of 38 C/Resolution 72 concerning educational and cultural institutions in the occupied Arab  territories” from the agenda of the Culture Commission.  (La Présidente poursuit en français)
