(15.1) PRESIDENT (Translation from the Russian):  At {ts meeting this morning, the General Committee considered how the General Confer-  ence should examine document 13 C/16, 1,e, the report by the Executive Board on the methods  of work of the Organization,
(15.2) Certain parts of the document have already been transmitted to the Administrative Com-  mission for consideration - items 18.1.1, 18.1.2, 18,1,3 and 18.1.4 of the agenda - and other  recommendations put forward by the General Committee concerning the organization of the work  of the present session have been adopted by the General Conference, There are still, however,  other important proposals submitted by the Executive Board, in particular proposals concern-  ing the methods of preparing the programme and budget,
(15.3) The General Committee considers that it would be desirable for some organ of the  Conference to examine document 13 C/16 as a whole, since all the proposals contained in that  document are interrelated. The General Committee therefore recommends that the Admint-  strative Commission be asked to study document 13 C/16 as a whole - both those parts of it  which have not yet heen placed on the agenda and those which have. However, in view of the  fact that some of the problems concerned may be outside the scope of purely administrative  matters, the General Committee requests that States be represented not only by members who  deal with administrative questions, but also by members speclalizing in those other questions,  The days on which the Administrative Commission is to discuss these questions will be  announced in the journal by the Chairman of the Programme Commission, so that delegations  can, if necessary, arrange to send more representatives to those meetings of the Administra-  tive Commission,
