Our Government feels that the programme of  Unesco in general is too ambitious, considering  the means at its disposal and the circumstances  of the time. We might even say that in this  programme there are several items which deal  with matters that are rather far removed from  the real purposes of the Organization. In history, names have  been given to different wars; we speak, for  instance, of the “Wars of the Roses”, and so on.  Today, if you wish to give a name to the quarrels  which are taking place between us, you should  say the “War of Words”. We believe we are in  agreement, but when it comes to practice we  find that everyone interprets the same words in  different and contrary ways. To give  them culture, we need centuries. Will life tarry  for us? Reality 1s at our door and has been  knocking since the day before yesterday.
