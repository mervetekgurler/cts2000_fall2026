12 November 1954 at 6 p.m.  Chairman : Mr. AL-HANI (Iraq)
(1) In place of the Director-General, who was  detained at another meeting, Mr. CHEVALIER  opened the proceedings and informed the Committee  that the General Conference had approved the  proposal by the Nominations Committee recom-  mending that one of the representatives of Iraq should  take the Chair at this Committee: Mr. Al-Hani, the  Iraq: delegate, was unanimously elect ed  Chairman  ITEM 10 OF REVISED AGENDA: ELECTION OF  VICE-CHAIRMEN AND RAPPORTEUR
(2) Having taken the Chair, Mr. AL-HANI suggested  that the delegates of Cambodia and Ecuador should  be elected Vice-Chairmen.
(3) Mr. CHIRIBOGA TERAN (Ecuador) thanked the  Chairman for his suggestion, but asked that the  delegate of the Dominican Republic should be  elected in his place
(4) Messrs. Hong Hoeung Doeung (Cambodia) and  Pannocchia Alvarez (Dominican Republic), having  been proposed by Mr McCULLOUGH (United States  of America), were unanimously elected  Vice-Chairmen.
(5) The CHAIRMAN then proposed that Mr. Hazoumé  (France) should be appointed Rapporteur.
(6) This proposal was adopted unanimously.
(7) The delegates of Cambodia, the Dominican  Republic and France thanked the Committee for  the honour 1t had just done them.
(8) In reply to a remark by Mr. HONG HOEUNG  DOEUNG {Cambodia) concerning the procedure for  studying the reports submitted to the Committee for  examination, the CHAIRMAN observed that that was  a question 10 be discussed at the next meeting.  The meeting rose at 6.45 p. m.
