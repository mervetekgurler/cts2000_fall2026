2.4 Madam President and fellow delegates, change is truly in the air - not only in terms of the  status of women and In the leadership of this Organization; we find ourselves on the threshold of  exciting new directions, which will inevitably affect the United Nationa system as a whole. The

2.5 Nothing quite like this exercise has ever been attempted or accomplished hefore by Unesco,  though there have been efforts ever since 1946 to give its programme a clearer focus.
