The traditional concept of 5 or 7 years secondary education was  perhaps a dangerous over-simplification, determined rather with reference to an academic  curriculum than to the needs of the person or the community. We plan to make our  secondary education programme more flexible and indeed very comprehensive, Special  teacher-training programmes are being introduced for all these essentially different areas  of secondary education,
24,14 Our own resources are indeed slender in comparison with the provision which  should be made to ensure basic human rights and economic security for our people. Under  the old laissez-faire concept of freedom, if you have not got a meal you are free to go  without; but our concept of the freedom of the individual has been restated in terms ofhu-  man worth and dignity, Already we have had some offers of assistance from agencies  such as the United Kingdom Voluntary Service Overseas project and the United States A gency for  International Development, and from our fellow Member States, the United Arab Republic  and Sweden, With this assistance we shall be able to help ourselves, and more effeciive-  ly help others,
24,15 I began by saying that mutual aid and service cannot be measured in terms of fin-  ancial resources and cited the record of my own country which has effectively participated  in the educational advancement of West Africa since over a century ago. I should like to  conclude on this note of interdependence and mutual aid which preserves at the same time  the integrity of the donor and the dignity of the recipient. The events of this century have  more firmiy established the economic resources of many of our fellow Member States, and  we rejoice with them; we do, however, trust that our own research and experience inedu-  cation over the next few years will not be without interest and value for our brethren with  whom we shall be proud to share any success we may achieve.
