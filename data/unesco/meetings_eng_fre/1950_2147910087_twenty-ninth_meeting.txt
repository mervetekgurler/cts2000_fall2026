TWENTY-NINTH MEETING  Friday, 16 June 1950, at 11 a.m.  Chairman: Dr. F. Benper (Netherlands)  Appropriation Resolution for 1951.  [1] Mr. WEITZ (Head, Bureau of Admini-  strative Management and Budget) presented the  draft document, which would be published as
5C/BUD/4.  Covering note.  Approved without comment.  Draft resolution.  [2] Mr. WEITZ (Head, Bureau of Admini-  strative Management and Budget) pointed out that  paragraphs 1, 2, 3 and 4 were self-explanatory.  With regard to paragraph 5, he explained that the  Appropriation Table added up to $8,300,000, but  that at the end of the Table there was a saving  of $100,000, accruing from the installation of the  local salary scales and from other changes in the  salaries and allowances system. It had not yet  been possible to make a detailed calculation of the  saving in question, not to indicate precisely where  it would take effect, but this would be done  shortly.  [3] Mr. WEEDEN (Australia) thought the Ap-  propriation Table would be valid only when both  the purpose and amount of each item was clearly  set forth in it. He suggested that paragraph 5  should be incorporated in paragraph 1.  [4] Sir Ronald ADAM (United Kingdom) sug-  gested the insertion in brackets, after the words  “Appropriation Table” in paragraph 1, of the  words “as amended in para-  graph 5 below”. Agreed.  ,  accordance with  [5] After some slight drafting amendments, the  Appropriation Resolution for 1951 was adopted.  Appropriation Table.  [6] In reply to Professor FANTAPPIE (Italy),  Mr. WEITZ (Head, Bureau of Administrative Man-  agement and Budget) explained that the figure  for “General Conference” had had to be increased  from $123,860 to $187,940, because of the addi-  tion of Spanish as a working language.  [7] Mr. WEITZ (Head, Bureau of Administrative  Management and Budget) pointed out that, two  evenings previously, the budget total had stood at  $8,214,000, plus $10,000 for the World Fed-  eration of United Nations Associations and  $50,000 for the dissemination of the Universal  Declaration of Human Rights. The Committee of  Eight having decided to leave it to the Director-  General to reduce that total by $74,000, this had  been done in accordance with the wishes expressed  by the Budget Committee; the alterations, which  brought the total budget figure down to the  required $8,200,000, would be submitted that  afternoon to the Programme and Budget Commis-  sion.  [8] Sir Ronald ADAM (United Kingdom) warm-  ly praised the Secretariat for the magnificent work  it had done.  Final draft report of the Budget Committee :  Report No. 2.  [9] The final draft report No. 2, to be published  as document 5C/BUD/3, was adopted without  comment.  The meeting rose at noon.
