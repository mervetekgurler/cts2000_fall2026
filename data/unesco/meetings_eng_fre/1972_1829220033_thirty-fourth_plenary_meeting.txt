16.1 Le DIRECTEUR GENERAL, -  Monsieur le Président, Mesdames, Messieurs, le texte de recommandation qui vous  est présenté au paragraphe 19 comporte des éléments trés importants, mais fort divers,  Parmi ces éléments, certains sont incontestablement de la compétence de 1'Unesco et le  Secrétariat s'efforcera de leur donner une traduction adéquate dans les programmes futurs;  mais il en est d'autres qui, au moins à première vue, me paraissent se situer en dehors  du domaine de compétence de l'Unesco, C'est pourquoi le libellé de ce texte appelle de ma  part quelques remarques,
16,2 D'abord, au paragraphe (d), il faudrait, je crois, remplacer - comme cela a déjà  été fait dans une autre résolution - les mots "organismes internationaux et intergouverne-  mentaux'' par les seuls mots ""organismes internationaux",
16.3 Je passe maintenant au paragraphe (f) où il est recommandé au Directeur général  d'élaborer un programme qui puisse "contribuer à éliminer les obstacles nulturels, sociaux,  et économiques qui empêchent le plein épanouissement de la personnalité de la femme".  Pour ce qui est des obstacles ''culturels", leur élimination est bien de la compétence de  l'Unesco et, en ce qui concerne l'élimination des obstacles ''sociaux'', l'Unesco, si elle ne  peut pas en avoir le monopole, peut tout au moins y contribuer. Je vois mal en revanche  par quels moyens l'Unesco pourrait contribuer à la réduction des obstacles économiques  qui empêchent le plein épanouissement de la personnalité de la femme, L'Unesco en effet  n'est pas une organisation à vocation économique, même si son action se situe - comme il  est évident - dans un contexte socio-économique, Je souhaiterais donc, Monsieur le Pré-  sident, que le mot "économiques" fat supprimé.
16.4 Mais c'est surtout ce qui est prévu au paragraphe (h) qui me paraft dépasser les pos-  sibilités de l'Unesco et être plutôt du ressort de l'Organisation internationale du travail,  I] s'agit en effet de "permettre que les femmes bénéficient de possibilités égales à celles  offertes aux hommes dans le domaine de l'emploi et des salaires, et que des postes de  haute responsabilité soient offerts aux femmes en tenant compte de leurs qualifications".  Par respect pour la compétence de la vénérable institution qu'est l'Organisation interna -  tionale du travail, je crois donc qu'il conviendrait de supprimer le paragraphe (h) qui, de  toute manière, se situe en dehors du domaine de compétence de l'Unesco et dépasse les  possibilités de son Secrétariat.

78,1 Mr, HARPER-SMITH (Comptroller):  Mr, President, the financial consequences of this resolution are somewhat complicated  and I hope that the way in which I explain them will make them clear to the delegates but if  they are not clear I hope that questions will be put so as to clarify those points. I will take  Section III of the resolution and deal with the paragraphs one by one; I think this is the most  logical way of explaining them,
78. 2 Paragraph 1 of Section III speaks of the balance due under the 50-year plan in an  amount of $2 million and it proposes that this amount should be offset against the corres-  ponding book surpluses for the period 1949-1958, What happened during those years 1949-
1958 is that the Conference only authorized the Director-General to spend part of the budget;  part of it was not to be spent because it was anticipated that it would not be received. Sub-  sequently the General Conference decided that the arrears due for those years should be  paid by annual instalments over fifty years and some of those instalments have been paid,  When they were paid they became cash surpluses and were distributed to Member States,  If they are not paid they do not become cash surpluses and will not be distributed to Mem-  ber States and that is what is meant by paragraph 1. The $2 million, if it is not received,  will not be available for distribution and therefore it is being offset against the possible  surpluses which would have arisen if it had been paid, This means that it does not repre-  sent a cash loss to the Organization, The money in question, the $2 million which is owing  was never spent,
78,3 When you come to paragraph 2 there are two sub-paragraphs. Sub-paragraph (i) re-  fers to the amount mentioned in paragraph 2 of Section I, that is $644, 877, This amount  is arrived at by apportioning the contribution of China for the year 1971 in two periods, one  for the period up to 28 October 1971 and the other for the period after 28 October 1971,
34  From the amount which relates to the period up to 28 October has been deducted certain  surpluses which had been apportioned to China but not paid to China because the contribu-  tions for those periods have not been paid in full, And the net figure due after giving credit  for those surpluses is $644,877, This represents a loss of revenue to the Organization,  This amount is required in’ order to finance the budget for 1971-1972, If it is not received  there has to'be an advance from the Working Capital Fund to make good that sum. The  whole of the budget for 1971-1972 is being spent; therefore that money must be recovered  from somewhere, In sub-paragraph (ii) reference is made to a balance being due from  three Member States, which are not recognized by those Member States as being due. If  you will refer to document 17 C/39, in paragraph 6 of that document, there is a note that |  says that according to Unesco's records, at 31 December 1971, the amount owing by these  three Member States was $1, 540, 344, That was the balance due at 31 December 1971; we  do not know what the balance will be at 31 December 1972, but it could be as high as  $1,700,000, This also represents a loss to the Organization, These are contributions  assessed to finance budgets which have been spent, If this sum is not received there is ,  again a loss of revenue to the Organization and it is necessary to advance this sum from  the Working Capital Fund, If, therefore, the debt is to be abolished, the sum in question  must be found from somewhere else,
78.4 Paragraph 3 deals with the possible method of recovering sums which are going to be  written off if the first Sections of the resolution are adopted, that is to say, it concérns the  recovery of $644,877 end a minimum of $1, 540, 344. The proposal in this paragraph is to  use budget surpluses which become available for distribution to Member States in the future,  to offset the cash loss due to these two amounts. This means in particular, Mr, President,  that on 1 January 1973 there would normally be a distribution of cask surpluses to Member  States. The amount estimated as being available at the present time is $950,000, This  sum would be apportioned to Member States and it would be deducted from their contribu-  tions for the next financial period. If paragraph 3 is adopted, that apportionment and sur-  render of surpluses will not take place. The sum will be transferred to the credit of the  special account to offset the sums due from China and the three other Member States, and  Member States will consequently have to pay a higher contribution next year,
78,5 These, Mr, President, are the consequences of the resolution; if my explanation is  not clear I will try to make it clearer by giving additional information, Thank you Sir,
