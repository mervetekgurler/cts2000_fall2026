Procedure to be followed for the election of  the Director General. (Item placed on the  agenda at the request of the delegate of the  United States of America.)  Mr. ALLEN (United States of America) pro-  posed that, if the unanimous consent of the  meeting could be obtained, an alteration should  be made in Section XVIII, Rule 99, of the Provi-  sional Rules of Procedure, to the effect that not  only the secret ballot on the nomination submitted  by the Executive Board for the post of Director-  General, but also the actual consideration of the  nomination by the General Conference should take  place in open meeting.  The DIRECTOR-GENERAL thought that the  adoption of such a procedure would be highly  undesirable, as it would be contrary to United  Nations practice and to the practice of Unesco  itself in any case where consideration of individual  names was involved. He pointed out, however,  that Rule 99 could be interpreted to mean that the  secret ballot should take place in public meeting.  Mr. HARDMAN (United Kingdom) reminded  the meeting that the Rules of Procedure had been  drawn up with the object of protecting not only  individuals, but also decisions of the Executive  Board and the General Conference itself; he also,  therefore, opposed the adoption of the procedure  proposed by Mr. Allen.  Mr. ALLEN (United States of America), while  stressing the fact that, in any case, the persons  present al the private meeting would possibly  not treat the debate which took place there as  confidential, withdrew his proposal.  It was agreed that, in accordance with the Rules  of Procedure, Section XVIII, Rule 99, consideration  of the nomination put forward by the Executive  Board for the post of Director-General should take  place at a private meeting, while the vote by secret  ballot should be taken at an open meeting.  The meeting rose at 11.15 a.m.
