The meeting rose at 7.10 p.m.  FOURTH MEETING  Saturday, 17 June 1950, at 9.30 a.m.  Chairman: Dr. E. Ronan WALKER (Australia)  Nominations for the new Budget Committee.  [1] The CHAIRMAN recalled that the Nomina-  tions Committee had to advise the General Con-  ference on the election of the eleven members of  the Budget Committee which was to meet one  week before the next Session. The Committee  would consist of eleven government representatives,  whose expenses would be met by their respective  governments, and its terms of reference would  be to examine the Budget Estimates presented  by the Director-General with a view to determin-  ing whether the estimates were reasonable and  accurate. It would consider the report and re-  commendations of the Executive Board, and as  soon as possible after the opening date of the  Conference, submit a report making such recom-  mendations as were necessary. It would also  examine new proposals, draft resolutions and  amendments presented by Member States and  having budgetary implications, and finally, tak-  ing into consideration the recommendations of  the Commissions and Committees, would submit  to the Plenary Session the Draft Appropriation  Resolution, the Appropriation Table for 1952 and  the General Report on the Budget.  The Chairman pointed out that it would be  desirable to include on the Committee those  countries which were best able to provide com-  petent persons to serve on such a technical body,  while preserving a reasonable geographical dis-  tribution.
