The CHAIRMAN stated that the Joint Com-  mittee of Fifteen was therefore to be considered  established.  Additional meeting of the Sub-Commission  on Natural Sciences.  The General Committee had received a request  for an additional meeting of the Sub-Commission  on Natural Sciences, and the CHAIRMAN thought  it would be practicable for such a meeting to be  held on the morning of 2 December.  Mr. ALLEN (United States of America) feared  that a similar situation might arise with other  Sub-Commissions; there might be a certain  danger in making an exception in favour of the  Natural Sciences Sub-Commission.    Mr. HARDMAN (United Kingdom) pointed out  that the Sub-Commission on Communication had  arranged a meeting for Friday, so that it might  not be as easy to agree to the proposed meeting  as it appeared.  Count Stefano JACINI (Italy) thought it quite  possible that other Sub-Commissions might be  faced by a similar necessity; but it would be inop-  portune for the Committee to withhold its assent,  particularly as most evenings were free.  The DIRECTOR-GENERAL pointed out that  there were eight or ten times, between 1 and
6 December, at which the Sub-Commission could  meet, without holding an evening meeting. He  stressed that the Secretary-General of the Confer-  ence should know the date chosen as soon as  possible.  Dr. NIELSEN (Denmark) emphasized that the  holding of such a meeting did not imply any  obligation on the part of the Programme and  Budget Commission to defer its own decisions.  The DIRECTOR-GENERAL said that that was  quite clear from the time-table of the Conference.
