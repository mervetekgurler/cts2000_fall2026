15 November 1954 at 4 p.m.  Chairman: Mr. KABIR (India)  later: Mr. BERREDO CARNEIRO ‘Brazil)
(1) Mr. KABIR (Chairman of the Nominations  Committee) opened the meeting and announced that  Dr. Parra-Perez (Venezuela), Chairman of the  Headquarters Committee, was unable to attend the  eighth session of the General Conference. The  Nominations Committee had therefore proposed that  the delegate of Venezuela be replaced by the  representative of another Latin American country,  Professor Berredo Carneiro, representative of  Brazil.
(2) Mr. Berredo Carneiro was elected by  acclamation.
(3) He was then invited to take the Chair in place  of Mr. Kahr.
(4) The CHAIRMAN thanked the members of the  Committee for electing him. He said that the agenda  of the meeting had now been exhausted but added  that during the current session of the General  Conference the Committee would be holding two or  three meetings, the times and dates of which would  be announced later.
(5) Mr. PHOTIADES (Greece) suggest: d that the    meeting approve the summary records of the  seventeenth session of the Committee, which had  just been distributed (documents 17 HQ/SR.1 (prov.)  and 17 HQ/SR. 2 (prov.))
