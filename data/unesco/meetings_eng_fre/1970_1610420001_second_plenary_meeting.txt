81,1 Mr, SHERMAN (Liberia):  Thank you Mr, President, distinguished delegates, ladies and ‘gentlemen, The Govern-  ment of Liberia believes in widening the membership of Unesco and of all the other Special-  ized Agéncies of the United Nations, We believe in extending the benefits of these Special-  ized Agencies to other countries and to every people. We believe in permitting them to  make their contribution to the educational, scientific and cultural development of mankind  and to the promotion of world peace, But we do not believe in the habitual abandonment of  principles once they have been accepted, In view of the existing rules governing the admis -  sion of non-United Nations members to Unesco or to any other Specialized Agencies, it is  the considered view of the Liberian delegation that unless the existing rules are amended  it is unrealistic to try to force the issue here at this Conference, We feel that this Confer-  ence cannot properly and reasonably be seized of the question of admitting a non-United  Nations member as the law provides,
81,2 Our position, therefore, is simple and clear; were we to be forced to vote, our posi-  tion would be "no". Let us first seek to amend'the rules and then try to negotiate anew.
81,3 My second question bears on the shortening of the term of office of the Executive  Board, I think we all admit that the work of Unesco is growing and expanding throughout  the world; the interests, particularly of developing countries, are increasing and the work  that is being done by Unesco is vital to those interests, We do'not believe that one country  ought to be permitted to stay as long as six or twelve years, So we will support the inclu-  sion on the Agenda of the desirability cf reducing the term of office. I thank you.
