SUMMARY RECORDS OF THE MEETINGS  OF THE GENERAL COMMITTEE  OF THE CONFERENCE  FIRST MEETING  Monday, 22 May 1950 at 5.30 p.m.  Chairman:  Nominations for the offices of Chairmen,  Vice-Chairmen and Rapporteurs of Commis-  sions and Committees [document 5C/NOM/2].  [1] After an exchange of views on the proposals  that the General Committee of the Conference had  received for the offices of Chairman, Vice-  Chairman and Rapporteur of the various Com-  missions and Committees of the Conference, it  was explained at the request of Sir John MAUD  (Chairman of the Executive Board) that, in  accordance with the provisions of Rules 30 and 47  of the Rules of Procedure, the lists prepared by  the Nominations Committee were not recommen-  dations but mere suggestions.  Organization of work of the Session [doc-  uments 5C/BUR/1 and 5C/BUR/1-Add. and  Corr.].  [2] On the CHAIRMAN’S recommendation, it  was decided to adopt provisionally the whole of  document 5C/BUR/1 (Proposed Organization of  Work of the Session), pending a detailed discus-  sion of the subject.  Terms of Reference of the Budget Committee  [document 5C/BUR/1Add. and Corr.].  [3] Without wishing to give the matter detailed  consideration, Sir John MAUD (Chairman, Ex-  ecutive Board) observed that the Executive Board  would have preferred the report of the Budget  Committee to be presented to the Conference by  the Programme and Budget Commission. He  asked that paragraph 3 (c) of document 5C/  Count STEFANQ Jacint (Italy)  BUR/1 - Add. & Corr.  accordingly.  (page 2) be amended  [4] The DEPUTY DIRECTOR-GENERAL had two  objections to Sir John's suggested change:
(1) The Budget Committee would then  become a mere sub-committee of the Programme  and Budget Commission, whereas he thought that  the original plan had been different;
(2) As the Budget Committee was required to  consider items involving budgetary implications  within the competence of the Administrative Com-  mission or of the Official and External Relations  Commission, but not of the Programme and Bud-  get Commission, it would hardly be possible to  submit the consolidated report to the latter Com-  mission alone.  [6] Sir John MAUD (Chairman, Executive Board)  said that it was not the Executive Board's intention  to weaken the Budget Committee by turning it  into a sub-committee of the Programme and Bud-  get Commission. He did not think that his sug-  gested change would have that effect. While  appreciating the validity of the second objection  made by the Secretariat, Sir John thought it not  impossible to present the consolidated report to the  General Conference sitting as the Programme and  Budget Commission—a solution which he thought  even offered important practical advantages.  [6] It was decided to amend paragraph 3 (c) to  bring it into line with the Executive Board's sug-  gestion, by inserting the words “through the  Programme and Budget Commission” after the  words “and submit to the Conference”.  The meeting rose at 6.30 p.m.
