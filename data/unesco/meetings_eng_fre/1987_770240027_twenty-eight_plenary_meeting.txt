2.1 Mr MARGAN (Yugoslavia), Chairman of the Executive Board:  Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, we have just concluded a private  meeting at which I had the privilege of submitting to the General Conference the  name of the candidate nominated and recommended by the Executive Board as  reported in document 24 C/NOM/17.
2.2 I wish now to recall, once again, that at the 125th session of the Board  we examined the procedure to be followed for the nomination of the Director-  General of the Organization. The procedure that we agreed upon was subsequently  adopted by the Board at the private session held on 1 October 1986. It has since  then been applied in accordance with the decision of.the Board. This procedure is  in conformity with the relevant provisions of the Constitution and the Rules of  Procedure of the General Conference.
2.3 It is also fully in conformity with Rule 5 3  of the Rules of Procedure of  the Executive Board which stipulates:  '(1) At least six months before the expiry of the term of office of the  Director-General or as soon as possible in case of vacancy at any  other time, the Executive Board shall invite Member States to  suggest, confidentially, the names of persons who might be con-  sidered for the post of Director-General, requesting them at the
878    same time to provide full biographical details regarding these  persons.
(2) The Executive Board shall consider in a private meeting all the  names so suggested, together with any proposed by members of the  Board, proviäed that no candidature shall be considered unless bio-  graphical details are available.
(3) The person to be nominated by the Executive Board shall be chosen  by secret ballot.
(4) The Chairman of the Board shall inform the General Conference of  the candidate nominated by the Board.'
2.4 It is in conformity with these provisions that, on 10 October 1986, the  Chairman addressed to the Member States a circular letter- (ref. CL/SCX/3700) by  which he invited the Member States to suggest to him, confidentially, if possible  before 2 April 1987, the names of persons who might be considered for the post of  Director-General. A draft version of this letter had been previously submitted to  the members of the Bureau for approval.
2.5 On 27 May 1987, the Chairman of the Board addressed to the Member States  a letter containing the list of candidates proposed as of that date, as well as  the names of the Member States which proposed or supported them. Subsequently the  Chairman sent to the Member States updated lists by ïetters dated 7, 22 and
24 September and 2 Gctober 1987.
2.6 The Executive Board held private meetings on 6, 7, 8, 9, 13, 14, 16, 17  and 18 October 1987 to consider item 9.1 of the agenda of its 127th session,  'Nomination of a candidate for the post of Director-General of Unesco', and took  the following decision:  'In accordance with the Constitution arid the Rules of Procedure, the  Board decided by secret ballot to present to the General Conference, at  its twenty--fourth session, the nomination of Mr Federico Mayor (Spain)  for the office of Director-General of Unesco.'  This decision is reflected in paragraph 2 of document 24 C/NOK/17.
2.7 In addition, in accordance with Rule 99 of the Rules of Procedure, the  Executive Board decided at its 127th session to submit to the General Conference  the contract between the Organization and the Director-General and the statute of  the Director-General.
