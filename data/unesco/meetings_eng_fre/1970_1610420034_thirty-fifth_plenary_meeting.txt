(1.1) The PRESIDENT (Translation from the Spanish):  I declare thé meeting open,
(1.2) Ladies and gentlemen, the General Committee of the Conference, deeply moved by the death  of General de Gaulle and sharing France's grief, decided this morning to convene this extra.  ordinary meeting, in order to pay a fitting tribute to the memory of the former President. At  this meeting, I, as President, was to express the feelings of the Conference as a whole, and there  were to be other speakers, representing the different geographical sectors, However, after the  meeting had been convened, we learnt that it was the General's last wish that there should be no  eulogies of him anywhere, We must respect this decision, in which he reveals himself, even in  death, in all his greatness. I therefore confine myself to inviting all present to stand and to pay  tribute in silence to thal great and illustrious man, France's son, but citizen of the world.  (All present rose and stood in silence for one minute, )
