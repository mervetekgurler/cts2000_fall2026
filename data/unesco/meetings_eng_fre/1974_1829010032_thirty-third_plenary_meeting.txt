(16.1) Mr. VARELA (World Peace Council) (Translation from the Spanish):  First of all ! should like to thank you, Mr, President, and this Assembly for being so kind as to allow  me to speak, We have already had the privilege of addressing the plenary meeting of this Conference once  before, on 28 October last,

(16,3) We also mentioned the many forms taken for some years past by the collaboration between the  World Peace Council and Unesco, both In respect of the basic problema which [ have just mentioned and  in respect of other questions of great importance, This certainly very fruitful collaboration was, for  example, reflected in International Education Year, International Book Year and World Population Year. (16,4) Such collaboration seems perfectly logical to our Council, since the striving towards peace {s, In  the last analysis, a quest for culture in its most profound and complete sense; since it involves the creation  of better conditions for the full development and material and apiritual advancement of every man and  woman and of all the nations,
