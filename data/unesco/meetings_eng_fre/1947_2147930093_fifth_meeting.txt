FIFTH MEETING Monday, 24 November 1947, at 3 p. m. Chairman : Dr. Esther C. BRUNAUER (United States of America) The Sub-Committee first took up consideration of the language question. It came to the conclusion that the decision should be taken on a higherlevel. All the delegations would have an opportunity to state their views on the subject in the light of the findings of the Sub-Committee. The question of the official languages was then considered, bearing in mind the practical difference between official languages and working languages. From the administrative and budgetary points of view, there was no obstacle to increasing the number of official languages. The Sub-Committee unani- mously agreed, however, that its conclusion on this subject could be the same as that arrived at with regard to working languages. Certain of the reser- vations which the Sub-Committee had made on the question of increasing the number of working lan- guages were founded not only on respect for budge- tary limitations but also on the desire to avoid crea- ting a precedent. The Sub-Committee then proceeded to a rapid review of the document drawn up as a result ofearlier discussions. It made certain slight alterations, to be taken into account when the document was published. The CHAIRMAN then stated that the work of the Sub-Committee was ended.
