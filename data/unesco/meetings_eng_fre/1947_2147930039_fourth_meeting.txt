A. — Educational, Scientific and Cultural Reconstruction Chairman : Appointment of Budget Sub-Commission. The CHAIRMAN : Our business at this meeting is the election of a Budget Sub-Commission. The fundamental document on procedure, in connexion with this and also in connexion with the Programme, is document 2 C/BUR/1. You will notice that on page 1 of document 2C/BUR/1, which was approved at a plenary meeting the other day, section 1.d. says that the Commission will establish a Budget Sub-Commission of not more than nine members to consider the budgetary implications of the programme proposals and resolutions of the Commission; to give budgetary review of the non- programme items of the budget and to report its recommendations to the Commission. The Sub- Commission shall meet at the call of the Chairman and shall sit continuously after the conclusion of the Commission's first review. We are, therefore, under instructions from the plenary meeting of the Conference to appoint a Budget Sub-Commission of nine members. I may, perhaps, again review what appear to me, and I think to the General Committee, to be the functions of this Budget Sub-Commission. I imagine that one of the aims of the Programme and qudget Commis- sion is to get the items for the 1948 Budget sepa- rately costed, and that this full Commission will constantly refer the items it has passed to the Budget Sub-Commission; it will be the job of the Sub- Commission to cost these items and to let us know in return, as soon as it can, what the cost of the items is, so that we can have a constant review, from a financial point of view, of the items we pass on the Programme. In this way the Programme for 1948 will be able to give a certain number of quite definite items which will have (to use the Director-General's Dr C. E. BEEBY (New Zealand) phrase) definite « price-tickets» attached to them in that Programme. That is the function of the Pro- gramme Commission as I see it. The Sub-Commission will as far as possible sit concurrently, or at any rate on the same days, with this Commission. You will notice that document
2C/BUR/1 shows in a time table a period ofthree to four days at the end ofour session when the Budget Sub-Commission can sit continously.
