The CHAIRMAN said that the business before the Committee was to proceed to the no- mination of seven candidates for the Executive Board, to fill the post left vacant by the resi- gnation of the Hon. A. Macleish (United States of America) and six outgoing members. The election was governed by Article V of the Constitution and by Rules 68, 69, 70 and 71 of the Rules of Proce- dure of the General Conference (these were read aloud by the secretary on the request of the dele- gate of Brazil). Sr. GUELL (Cuba) pointed out that, as Unesco was essentially a democratic institution, respecting the principle of equality between nations, the study of the questions with which it was concerned and preli- minary information regarding those questions should not be reserved to a privileged group, lest the future action of the Organization be prejudiced. Many countries had done excellent work for Unesco but were not represented on the Executive Board. While he paid tribute to the good work of the candidates for re-election, nothing should be done to prevent delegates of other nations from filling posts which allow them to become fully acquainted with whole of the Organization’s work. The CHAIRMANrecalled that, under the Rule of Procedure, all members of the Executive Board were eligible on the same terms as other candidates. He suggested that, in conformity with Rule 71, they should proceed to the scrutiny of candidates to replace the member whose resignation they regretted ; he called on the delegate of the United States of America. M. BENTON (United States of America) was sure he spoke for them all in expressing their keen regret at the resignation of Mr. MacLeish.
