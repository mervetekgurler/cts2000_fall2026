3.1 Mr. KIRPAL (India):  Mr, President and fellow delegates, speaking on behalf of the Chairman of the Drafting  Committee, the Indian Minister of Education, who left for New Delhi this morning, I wish  to thank the Director-General for his sound and constructive suggestions in regard to the  form and logical order of document 16 C/DR/PLEN/16, These suggestions improve the  text prepared by the Drafting Committee and I believe would be generally acceptable, The  interpretation of some expressions in the draft resolution and the explanations concerning  implementation offered by the Director-General are also acceptable and we appreciate his  concern to give more lucidity and substance to the draft resolution,
3.2 In the Drafting Committee, Mr, President, the delegate of India made no reservations  in regard to voting on any part of the proposed resolution. We shall therefore vote for all  the paragraphs of the draft prepared by the Committee subject to what I have said about the  Director-General's suggestions and one additional exception. The one exception is the new  version of paragraph 30(d) proposed by the distinguished delegate of Trinidad and Tobago  in document 16 C/DR/PLEN/19 after consultation in an improvised group during the brief  interval this morning, We shall vote for this version which replaces the amendment tabled  earlier by the delegate of Afghanistan, I know that Dr, Rao was favourably inclined towards  the amendment of Afghanistan and would have put it to the Drafting Committee if it had been
34  received earlier than the final stage of the Committee's work when no alterations could be  considered,
3.3 The amendment proposed by the delegate of Trinidad and Tobago is substantially the  same as the proposal of the delegate of Afghanistan, and gives greater clarity and, Ibelieve,  larger acceptability to the ideas and procedures contained in the Drafting Committee's reso-  lution. I am glad that the delegate of Afghanistan has now withdrawn his amendment, and
1 hope very much that the delegate of France will do likewise, The amendment of Trinidad  and Tobago ensures the implementation of what we decide today,
3.4 Mr, Presideni, the most important features of the present resolution are increased  emphasis on action and implementation, and a significant departure from the merely high-  sounding formulations of the past that were seldom substantiated, Paragraph (b) of the  draft proposed by the delegate of Trinidad and Tobago calls upon the Executive Board to  take certain practical measures in implementation of this resolution, We hope that these  measures can be completed by the 88th session of the Board, and in no case later than the
89th session, Consequently when our General Conference meets again at its seventeenth  session it will receive a full report on the implementation of the resolution.
3,5 Finally, Mr, President, on behalf of the delegation of India, may I appeal to you and  through you to the distinguished delegates assembled here, that we vote in a spirit of unani-  mity of ideals, objectives and instrumentalities, even if a particular formulation of words  appears inadequate or imperfect to some of us, The pursuit of peace is the concern of all,  and no one in this assembly can possibly defend racialism and colonialism in any form,  The resolution does not attack the zutonomy of non-governmental organizations, They are  and must remain autonomous by the very definition of their structures and functions. But  Unesco also has the right to choose its own associations and our Organization cannot  possibly associate itself in any form with an organization that is opposed to our own basic  principles and fundamental objectives, On behalf of the twenty-three countries sponsoring  the original draft resolution, Mr, President, may I inake a fervent appeal for the adoption  of this resolution in a spirit of unanimity and for its implementation by positive deeds and  actions to eliminate colonialism, racialism, the practice of apartheid and aggression in  all forms, Thank you, Mr, President,
