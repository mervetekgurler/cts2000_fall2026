(1,2) The first item on today's agenda is the continuation of the discussion on Item 15,6,1 -  ""Unesco's tasks in contributing to peace, peaceful co-operation, and living peacefully together,  among States with different economic and social systems", As a result of the discussion of this  item of the agenda at the plenary meeting on Wednesday, 4 November, a drafting committee was  set up to prepare a draft resolution acceptable to, and in accordance with the wishes of, all  delegates, This drafting committee, which was appointed by the General Committee of the Con-  ference, was composed of representatives of Argentina, India, the Union of Soviet Socialist  Republics, the United Arab Republic, the United Kingdom and the United States of America, It  met yesterday under the Chairmanship of the Head of the delegation of Sweden, Mr. Ragnar Lund. 2.1 Mr, LUND (Sweden):  Mr. President, I have the honour to preside over the drafting committee which you set  up for the purpose of attempting to prepare a draft resolution which would be acceptable to  both groups of delegations which sponsored the draft resolutions DR/71 and DR/72 of
2.2 As has been mentioned, Argentina, India, the United Arab Republic, the United  Kingdom, USSR and the United States of America were represented in the committee, un-  der the chairmanship of the delegate of Sweden. The committee held two meetings on
5 November, and I am happy to be in a position to report to you that, after prolonged dis-  cussions and thanks to the sincere co-operation of all its members, agreement was reached  on the text of a draft resolution entitled " Unesco's tasks in contributing to peace, peaceful  co-operation, and living peacefully together, among States with different economic and  social systems." This text is reproduced in document 13 C/41, which has already been  made available to you in the four working languages of the General Conference,
2,3 The draft resolution reads as follows, and of course the text in each language in  equally authentic.  Draft resolution submitted by the drafting committee  Unesco's tasks in contributing to peace, peaceful co-operation, and living peacefully  together, among States with different economic and social systems  The General Conference,  Guided by the provisions of Unesco's Constitution proclaiming that the basic purpose of  the Organization is to " contribute to peace and security by promoting collaboration  among the nations through education, science and culture",  Recognizing that for the development of science, culture and education, as well as for  international collaboration in these fields conditions of peaceful and good neighbourly  relations among States are necessary,  Considering that Unesco by its own means and efforts and within the sphere of its com-  petence, while contributing directly to improving the well-being of the peoples and  making an important contribution to the easing of international tensions, the securing  of universal peace and the promotion of good neighbourly relations, can and should  develop its activities in this direction to the fullest possible extent,  Recalling resolution 3.51 of its ninth session, inviting Member States to direct their  attention to gaining recognition for the ideas of living peacefully together, and resolu-  tion 8,1, on "peaceful and neighbourly relations", adopted at its eleventh session,  Calls upon Member States to be guided in their relations with one another by the prin.  ciples of living peacefully together and peaceful co-operation, having regard to mutual  respect and benefit, non-aggression, respect for each other's sovereignty, equality  and territorial integrity, non-intervention in one another's internal affairs, the broaden-  ing of international co-operation, the reducing of tensions and the settling of differences
813    XXVIIT l'lenury meetings / Séances plénières  and disputes among States by peaceful means, as expreasecd in resolution 1236 (XII) of  the General Assembly of the United Nations,  Iequests that the Director-General and the Executive Board in the carrying out by all  Departments of the Secretariat of their activities with respect to education, science  and culture, and the International Co-operation Year, should be guided by the spirit of  this resolution and assist the application of the principles stated above which will help  to eliminate the threat of world war, secure the final abolition of colonialism, improve  the well-being of the peoples and create more favourable conditions for the development  of education, science and culture, as well as international collaboration inthese fields,
2.4 It is my sincere hope, Mr, President, that the draft resolution in its present form will  also prove acceptable to the General Conference.
