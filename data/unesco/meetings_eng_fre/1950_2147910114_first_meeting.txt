SUMMARY RECORDS OF THE MEETINGS  OF THE NOMINATIONS COMMITTEE  FIRST MEETING  Monday, 22 May 1950, at 4.30 p.m.  Chairman: Dr. E. RonaLp WaArxer (Australia)  Election of Chairman.  [1] At the proposal of Mr. HARDMAN (United  Kingdom), Dr. Walker (Australia) was unani-  mously elected Chairman of the Committee.  Nominations for the offices of President and  seven Vice-Presidents of the General Confer-  ence [document 5C/NOM/2].  Office of President of the General Conference.  [2] Mr. DELBOS (France) proposed the election  of H. E. Professor Guido Gonella, Italian Minister  of Education and Head of the Italian delegation.  He was well aware that Professor Gonella’s official  duties would oblige him to absent himself on  frequent occasions, but he hoped that he would  accept the Presidency, subject to his right to be  replaced whenever necessary.  Mr. SARGEANT (United States of America)  warmly seconded that nomination.  [3] Professor GONELLA (Italy) expressed deep  appreciation of the honour shown him, but  regretted being unable to accept, as he was afraid  that he would not be able to carry out his duties  as President sufficiently regularly. He recom-  mended in his place H. E. Count Stefano Jacini,  member of the Executive Board, who, having  participated in Unesco’s work since the Beirut  Session of the Conference, would certainly be in a  position to occupy the important office of Presi-  dent. Adopted by acclamation.  Offices of Vice-Presidents of the General Conference.  [4] Sir Sarvepalli RADHAKRISHNAN (India)  proposed the nomination of representatives of the  following countries: Brazil, Egypt, the United  States of America, France, Pakistan, the United  Kingdom and Venezuela.  Unanimously adopted.  [6] The CHAIRMAN announced that the fore-  going nominations would be submitted for  approval to the Plenary Session of the General  Conference.  The meeting rose at 4.50 p.m.
