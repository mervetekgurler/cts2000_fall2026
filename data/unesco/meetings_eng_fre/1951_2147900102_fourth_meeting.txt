[5] Mr. BRULL (Cuba) was in favour of increasing the membership of the Budget Committee. He made the suggestion for various reasons, the principal one being that the number of Unesco’s Member States had increased to 64. In the interests of a fair geo- graphical distribution, it was essential that a larger number of countries should be represented on such an important body as the Budget Committee. He therefore proposed that the membership of that Committee should be increased from 11 to 15. [6] The CHAIRMAN sympathized with the proposal of the delegate of Cuba, but pointed out that, under Rule 89 of the Rules of Procedure, the Committee had no power to decide upon such an increase. The matter fell within the competency of the Plenary Conference. He asked the Chairman of the Budget Committee, Mr. Bender, to give the Committee his opinion. [7] Mr. BENDER (Netherlands), speaking as Chair- man of the Budget Committee, thought that, as the number of Unesco’s Member States had increased to 64, it would be in the interests of fair geographical distribution to increase the membership of the Bud- get Committee from 11 to 15. It was not, however, for the Nominations Committee to take a final deci- sion on the number, and he therefore suggested that the Nominations Committee should nominate
15 countries provisionally and, if the Plenary Confer- ence reversed the decision and resolved on a smaller number, two or three countries would then have to be dropped. [8] In nominating 15 countries, the following prin- ciples should be borne in mind: (1) the desirability of continuity, which would make it advisable to maintain five or six of the former members in office; (2) geographical distribution should be as equitable as possible; (3) those countries paying the largest contributions should be represented on the Budget Committee. [9] On the basis of 15 members, and bearing the above principles in mind, Mr. Bender tentatively sug- gested the following countries: Belgium, Brazil, France, India, Iran, Liberia, Mexico, New Zealand, Pakistan, Switzerland, Turkey, Uruguay, United Kingdom, United States of America, Yugoslavia. [10] Mr. Bender said that, having had the honour of being elected Chairman of the Budget Committee for three successive sessions of the General Confer- ence, he did not wish to stand for 1952.
