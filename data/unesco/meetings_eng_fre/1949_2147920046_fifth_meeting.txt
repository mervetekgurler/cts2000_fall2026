The DEPUTY DIRECTOR-GENERAL said that the Beirut Conference had voted that action be taken on the problem of Arab refugees and that an allowance of $175,000 be made in the budget for that purpose. This amount, however, had proved insufficient and, on 29 September 1949, the Programme and Budget Commission had submitted to the Executive Board a resolution in which it requested that a further sum of $30,000 be transferred from the Emergency Fund for 1949. The Executive Board, though in full sympathy with the cause in question, had found itself unable to act on the resolution in view of paragraph 3 (c) of the Appropriation Resolution for 1949 (doc- ument 3C/110, Vol. II, page 39). It had there- fore re-submitted the resolution, in a slightly amended form, to the Programme and Budget Commission for action by the General Conference which was the only body competent to authorize the transfer in question.
