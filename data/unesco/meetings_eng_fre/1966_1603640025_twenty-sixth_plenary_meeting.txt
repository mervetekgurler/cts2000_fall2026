10.2 Unesco's rôle in the building of peace is distinctive. It should pursue this special  rôle with single-minded resolution, knowing that if It does not do this job no other agency  will, Unesco's rôle, which is served by no other international agency, is the promotion of  non-political co-operation and collaboration in the fields of education, science and culture,  Other agencies of the United Nations have functions that are also indispensable ; but they  are different functions. Unesco must realize that if it takes on other people's jobs, it will’  hurt its capacity to do what it alone can do. It can invite lack of faith in the value of its  own activities when it seeks to substitute other activities, or exhausts its energies by  tuking on responsibilities that other agencies are better able to fulfil,
10.3 In performing its distinctive réle, a primary instrument available to Unesco is the  stimulation and organization of practical intellectual collaboration, across the frontiers,  ¢mong the learned men and civic leaders who work in Unesco's field of competence, Inter-  national understanding requires the practice of co-operation among those whose major oc-  cupation in life is to search for understanding - research, teaching, and the practice of the  arts and sciences,
10.4 The development and promotion of education, science and culture are distinctive  means by which Unesco secks to create a climate or condition for peace. We might well  recall, as Jean-Jacques Rousseau reminded mankind, that there are some kinds of peace  that are intolerable, and some kinds of social order that are merely names for slavery,  "You will say", Rousseau wrote, "that the despot guarantees civil tranquility to his sub-  jeets ... but ... what does it profit them, if this very tranquillity is one of their mis-  fortunes? Men also live tranquilly in dungeons ; is that enough to make prisoners content ?",  Rousseau inquired, Such a peace and such a tranquillity are ignoble and explosive. Unesco  can build a just peace 1; enriching the qualities of man's life and by promoting, throughout  the world, respect for justice, the rule of law, individual human rights and the fundamental  freedoms affirmed by the United Nations Charter, In furthering education, science and  culture, Unesco indeed is not only building a path to peace - it is practising peace in the  here and now and demonstrating in the concrete what peace is, and what peace is for.
10.5 To help create a climate of peace, Unesco must resist firmly the temptation to em-  bark on excursions into international political issues, It should avoid war-like words which  are uttered in the name of peace. It should avoid statements which divide mankind neatly  into two castes: the persecuted and the persecutors ; the virtuous and the wicked, These  are not distinctions worthy of men who have thought seriously about the human condition or  who know elementary facts of human life, Unesco, indeed, cannot build peace - it will do  quite the opposite - if it seeks to impose ideological unity or cultural uniformity, It should,  instead, concentrate on well-defined projects and activities, capable of arousing widespread,  and honest support and capable of practical realization,

10,6 Unesco should not be looked upon as vested with a monopoly of initiative or responsi-  bility in its assigned field of activity, The goal is International co-operation, not direction  or management by Unesco, Our Organization must recognize that intellectual collaboration  and co-operation take place outside Unesco's framework - for example, through regional  and bilateral programmes - and that this too serves the Unesco idea, At the same time,  we also must recognize that the other agencies of the United Nations system, in pursuing  thelr own tasks in laying and enlarging the groundwork for peace, will find themselves  inevitably, to some extent, in an area of common interest, as we shall also. 10,7 Finally, it would be a mark of wisdom, we believe, to distinguish sharply between  Uncsco's potential frame of reference and what Unesco, at any given time, can and should  attempt to do.
