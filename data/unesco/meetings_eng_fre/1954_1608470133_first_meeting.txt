12 November 1954, 6.10 p.m.  Chairman : Mr. AWAD (Egypt)  OPENING OF TIE MEETING AND NOMINATION OF A CHAIRMAN
(1) Mr, SABA (Legal Adviser) declared the meeting  open and requested members of the Legal Committee  to elect their chairman, The Nominations Committee  had put forward the name of Mr. Mohamed Awad  Mohamed, leader of the Egyptian delegation. In the  absence of any observations, the choice of Mr. Awad  was automatically accepted.    (Mr. Awad took the chair.)
(2) The CHAIRMAN, after expressing his thanks for  the honour done him, proposed that the meeting be  adjourned and that the General Conference be  informed of this decision.  The meeting rose at 6.20 p.m,  SECOND MEETING
17 November 1954, at 10.30 a.m.  Chairman: Mr, AWAD (Egypt)  ELECTION OF A VICE-CHAIRMAN AND A RAPPORTEUR
(1) The CHAIRMAN said that the first business of  the Committee was the election of a Vice-Chairman  und of a Rapporteur,
(2) Mr. HUZAYIN (Egypt) proposed, and the  proposal was duly seconded, that Mr, Jaramillo  Arango (Colombia) should be elected Vice-Chairman  of the Committee.
(3) It was also proposed, and the proposal was  seconded, that Mr. BAUGNIET (Belgium) be elected  Rapporteur,
(4) Both these proposals were adopted,
(5) Mr, BAUGNIET (Belgium) thanked the Committee  for the honour thus done him. However, he could  agree to act as Rapporteur only provided that that    would not prevent him from speaking freely during  the discussions.
(6) The CHAIRMAN said that anyone acting as  Rapporteur was rendering a service to the Committee.  The Rapporteur would of course retain complete  liberty to put forward an independent point of view.
