THIRTEENTH MEETING  Wednesday, 14 June 1950, at 9.45 p.m.  Chairman: Dr. TARA CHAND (India)  Report of the Administrative Commission to  the General Conference [document 5C/  ADM/25].  [1] Mr. RODRIGUEZ LARRETA (Uruguay) and  Mr. GUIGNOLET (France) deeply deplored the  delay in the distribution of the French text, but  Mr. de VISSCHER (Belgium) pointed out that the  delay could not be ascribed entirely to the Secre-  tariat, whose task had been considerably com-  plicated by the prolonged discussions of the  Commission and the resulting numerous changes  in the programme of meetings.  FOURTEENTH  [2] Miss LAISE (United States of America) had  noticed several superficial errors in the English  text, which altered the meaning, whereupon Sir  Ben THOMAS (United Kingdom) suggested that  the “Roseman Committee” meet at 9 a.m. the  following day to revise the text which could be  presented to the Commission in the afternoon.  That proposal, seconded by Mr. RODRIGUEZ  LARRETA (Uruguay) and Dr. BEAGLEHOLE  (New Zealand), was adopted.  The meeting rose at 10 p.m.  MEETING  Thursday, 15 June 1950, at 3 p.m.  Chairman: Dr. Tara CHAND (India)  Report of the Administrative Commission to  the General Conference (continued) [docu-  ment 5C/ADM/25].  [1] After putting the final touches to the draft  report revised by the “Roseman Committee’, the  Commission adopted the final text as given in  document 5C/ADM/25 (rev.) and in Section IV  of document 5C/Resolutions.  [2] Miss LAISE (United States of America)  thanked the Chairman, Dr. Tara Chand, and the  lapporteur, Mr. W.G. St.C. Smith, on behalf of  the Commission.  The meeting rose at 4 p.m.
